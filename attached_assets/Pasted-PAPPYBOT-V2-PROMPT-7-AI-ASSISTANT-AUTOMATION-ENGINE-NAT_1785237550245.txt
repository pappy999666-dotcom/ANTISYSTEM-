---

OBJECTIVE

Build an intelligent AI Assistant that becomes the personal administrator for each WhatsApp session.

The AI should understand natural language, translate requests into bot actions, schedule tasks, answer questions about the user's own bot configuration, and automate repetitive work.

The AI must always respect the existing permission system.

---

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The AI is an assistant, not a replacement for the command system.

Every AI action must internally execute existing services and commands rather than duplicating business logic.

The AI should:

Understand

↓

Plan

↓

Validate Permissions

↓

Generate Execution Plan

↓

Execute Existing Services

↓

Report Result

---

Examples include:

Groq

Future providers

---

ACTIVATION

The AI responds only when:

The sender is the Session Owner or SUDO.

AND

AI is enabled.

AND

The configured AI prefix is used.

Example:

pappy mute the group tomorrow at 2 PM

pappy send this message next Friday

pappy open the group in one hour

pappy warn @user

pappy create a poll

The AI should never execute commands for ordinary users.

---

NATURAL LANGUAGE ENGINE

The AI should interpret natural language requests and convert them into structured actions.

Examples:

"Close the group at 10 PM."

"Open the group tomorrow morning."

"Send this image every Friday."

"Kick anyone who joins twice."

"Enable AntiLink."

"Disable Welcome."

"Show runtime statistics."

Do not hardcode phrases.

Use intent detection and planning.
